What TenXHouse is—and is not

Clear boundaries protect the brand and the customer.

TenXHouse is

  • A host platform for the confirmed companies
  • A membership and business-presence proposition
  • A route to approved ecosystem benefits
  • A useful source of entrepreneur information
  • A curator of relevant events

TenXHouse is not

  • A coworking or hot-desk provider
  • A meeting-room rental business
  • A public office-space rental operation
  • A venue-hire proposition
  • A generic list of discounts

Zoho Creator-led joining workflow

One application should drive onboarding, portal access and operational records.

HubSpot remains the CRM and marketing-consent source. Zoho Creator manages the application, membership, benefits, events and member portal. ClickUp receives actionable tasks; SharePoint stores controlled documents.

Apply

Prospect opens an approved Creator application; HubSpot receives source and contact context.

Verify

Creator validates fields and documents; ClickUp gets a task only when human action is needed.

Activate

After approval and the confirmed payment step, Creator activates membership and portal access.

Serve and renew

Benefits, events, support, renewal and operational status remain in Creator; marketing stays in HubSpot.
