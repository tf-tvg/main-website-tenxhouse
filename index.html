Positioning boundary

This must not feel like a generic coworking website.

TenXHouse can use physical presence where appropriate, but the public offer is not built around desk rental, venue hire or walk-in office space. The centre of gravity is business credibility, operating support and ecosystem access.

TenXHouse is

  • A business-presence and support platform
  • A membership-led growth ecosystem
  • A route into hosted-company and partner value
  • A source of practical entrepreneur information
  • A controlled event and registration pathway

TenXHouse is not

  • A generic coworking space
  • A hot-desk catalogue
  • A meeting-room rental business
  • A public venue-hire offer
  • A loose discount marketplace
