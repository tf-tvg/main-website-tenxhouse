Privacy Policy (POPIA)

This privacy notice explains how TenXHouse handles website enquiries and may be updated as systems and services develop.

Information we may collect

TenXHouse may collect information you choose to share when you contact us, including your name, business name, email address, mobile number, enquiry type and message details.

Why we collect it

We use enquiry information to respond to requests, recommend the relevant TenXHouse service, prepare onboarding steps and share relevant information about services you asked about.

How it may be stored

Information may be stored in approved business tools such as email, CRM, Zoho Creator, Zoho Forms, Microsoft 365 or similar operational systems.

Who may access it

Access should be limited to authorised TenXHouse or TenOne Venture Group team members and approved service providers who need the information to respond to or manage your enquiry.

Retention

Information should be kept only for as long as needed for enquiry handling, client onboarding, service delivery, legal compliance or legitimate business record keeping.

Correction or deletion requests

You may request correction or deletion of your personal information by contacting TenXHouse through the contact details on this website.

Consent wording

When you send an enquiry, you agree that TenXHouse may use your information to respond to the enquiry and share relevant information about its services. You can request removal from communications.
