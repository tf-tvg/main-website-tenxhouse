Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers for entrepreneurs, creatives, partners and sponsors considering TenXHouse.

What is TenXHouse?

TenXHouse helps entrepreneurs, creatives and local businesses in Linden look credible, stay organised and grow with practical support.

Can I use TenXHouse as a business address?

You can enquire about professional business address support. Required documents, package limits and address use rules are confirmed before onboarding.

Are bookkeeping and tax services included?

Bookkeeping support may be available, but exact transaction limits, SARS submissions, tax advice and annual financial statement scope must be confirmed in writing.

How do I become a partner or sponsor?

Use the partner or sponsor enquiry route and tell us what value you can bring to entrepreneurs, creatives or local business owners.

How do event RSVPs work?

Join the event interest list and TenXHouse will share dates, capacity and RSVP details when sessions open.
